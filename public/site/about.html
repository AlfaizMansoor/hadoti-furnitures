About Us

Solid wood furniture, handcrafted in Sanganer, Jaipur.

Hadoti Furnitures is a family-run furniture workshop based in Sanganer, Jaipur, Rajasthan. Every piece we sell is built in our own manufacturing branch, from seasoned solid wood, using honest joinery and calm, warm finishes.

How We Work

  • Each design is cut, joined, sanded and finished by our own craftsmen — nothing is outsourced.
  • We price honestly: the same piece, the same price, whether you visit the showroom or order online.
  • Custom sizes and finishes are welcome; speak to us before placing your order.
