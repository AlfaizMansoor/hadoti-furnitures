Return & Refund Policy

Because every piece is handcrafted to order, here's exactly how cancellations, damage and refunds work.

Each piece of Hadoti Furnitures is solid wood, built or finished in our own workshop specifically for your order. This means our return process looks a little different from off-the-shelf furniture, but we stand fully behind the quality of what we build.

Order Cancellations

  • Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they enter production or are dispatched. Contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel.
  • Once a piece has entered production or has been dispatched, it can no longer be cancelled, as materials and workshop time have already been committed.

Returns

Because most pieces are made or finished to order, we are not able to accept returns for a change of mind once production has started. This is standard practice for made-to-order solid wood furniture and lets us keep our pricing honest for everyone.

Damaged or Defective on Arrival

  • If your order arrives damaged or with a manufacturing defect, inform us within 48 hours of delivery with clear photos of the issue and the packaging.
  • We will arrange a repair, replacement or part-replacement of the affected component at no extra cost, once the issue is verified.
  • Please avoid using or attempting to repair the affected piece yourself before our team has reviewed it — this helps us process your claim faster.

1-Year Warranty

  • The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and joinery. It does not cover normal wear, accidental damage, misuse, or changes in wood tone from sunlight and humidity over time, which are natural to solid wood.
  • To make a warranty claim, contact us with your order details and photos of the issue.

Refunds

  • Approved refunds for cancelled or unfulfilled orders paid via Razorpay are credited back to the original payment method, typically within 5–7 business days of approval.
  • Refunds for orders paid by UPI or bank transfer are sent back to the same account or UPI ID once confirmed, within the same timeframe.
  • You will receive a confirmation once your refund has been initiated.

Custom Orders

Custom sizes, finishes or design changes are made specifically for you and are non-returnable except in the case of a manufacturing defect covered under warranty above.

Need Help With an Order?

Reach out with your order number and we'll sort it out as quickly as we can.
