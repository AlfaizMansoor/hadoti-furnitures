Shipping Policy

How and when your order reaches you, from our workshop to your door.

Every piece is built to order or dispatched from our manufacturing branch in Sanganer, Jaipur. Because each item is solid wood and often made or finished after your order is placed, delivery timelines are a little longer than mass-produced furniture — we'd rather take the time to get it right.

Delivery Coverage

We deliver across India. Remote or hard-to-reach locations may take a few extra days, and our team will let you know if that applies to your pincode before your order is confirmed.

Delivery Charges

  • Delivery charges are calculated per piece and shown clearly at checkout before you pay — there are no hidden charges added later.

Dispatch and Delivery Timelines

  • In-stock pieces are typically dispatched within 3–5 business days of order confirmation.
  • Made-to-order or custom-finish pieces take longer to build; our team will share an estimated timeline when you place the order.
  • Once dispatched, most cities receive their order within 5–10 business days depending on distance and accessibility.
  • You can track the status of your order from the order confirmation page at any time.

Packaging

Every piece is wrapped and padded in-house before it leaves our workshop, the same way we would pack it for our own showroom transfers, to keep it protected in transit.

Receiving Your Order

  • Please inspect the packaging at the time of delivery. If a package looks visibly damaged, note it with the delivery partner before accepting, and inform us the same day with photos.
  • Standard delivery is to your doorstep. Assembly or in-home placement, where needed, can be arranged — ask our team when you order.
  • Someone should be available to receive the order on the estimated delivery date; our team will coordinate a suitable time with you beforehand.

Delays

Occasionally, weather, regional logistics disruptions or festival-season demand can push delivery beyond the estimated window. If your order is delayed, we will proactively update you with a revised timeline.

Questions About Your Delivery

For anything specific to your order — an address change, a preferred delivery date or a delay — reach out to us directly and we'll help.
