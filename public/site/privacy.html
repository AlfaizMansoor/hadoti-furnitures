Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Hadoti Furnitures respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you browse our website or place an order, and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Your name, mobile number, email address and delivery address when you place an order or send an enquiry.
  • Order details, including the products purchased and payment status.
  • Basic browsing information such as the pages you view, used to improve the website.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process, deliver and service your orders.
  • To respond to enquiries and provide after-sales support, including warranty claims.
  • To send order updates and, with your consent, occasional information about new collections.

Payments

Online payments are processed by our authorised payment partner. We do not store your card details, UPI credentials or bank information on our servers.

Sharing of Information

We share your details only with delivery partners and service providers who help us complete your order, or where disclosure is required by law. We never sell your personal information.

Data Security and Retention

Your information is stored securely and retained only for as long as it is required for order records, warranty service and legal compliance.

Cookies and Local Storage

Your cart, wishlist and recently viewed items are stored in your browser so that your selections remain available on your next visit. You can clear them at any time from your browser settings.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details below.
